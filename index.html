About Me

Hi there! Welcome to DoneeerCorporation.

I am a solo indie developer starting my journey in game and application creation. My goal is to build interesting, fun games for the Google Play Store and web browsers, alongside practical utility apps that make daily tasks easier.

Being an independent creator allows me to experiment with unique concepts and pour my creativity straight into every project. Since I'm just starting out, every download, feedback, and review means the absolute world to me!

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

DoneeerCorporation ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and disclose information when you use our games, applications, websites, and related services.

1. Information We Collect

We do not directly collect or store personal information such as your name, address, email address, or phone number.

Local Data: Our games and applications may store game progress, settings, and other data locally on your device using technologies such as Unity PlayerPrefs, local storage, or similar methods. This information remains on your device unless you choose to remove it.

Advertising Data: Our games, applications, and websites may display advertisements through third-party advertising providers, including Google AdMob and Google AdSense. These services may collect information such as your advertising identifier, IP address, browser type, device information, cookies, and other technical identifiers in order to:

  • Display relevant advertisements.
  • Measure advertising performance.
  • Prevent fraud and abuse.
  • Improve advertising quality.

2. Cookies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies. These may be used by Google AdSense and other third-party partners to personalize advertisements, measure performance, and improve user experience.

3. How We Use Information

The limited information collected through our services is used only to:

  • Provide and maintain our games, applications, and website.
  • Display advertisements.
  • Analyze usage and improve our products.
  • Protect against fraud, abuse, and technical issues.

4. Data Retention

We do not maintain servers that store your personal information. Local game data remains on your device until you remove it by clearing application data, browser storage, or uninstalling the application.

5. Third-Party Services

Our products may use third-party services including, but not limited to:

  • Google AdMob
  • Google AdSense
  • Google Play Services

These services have their own privacy policies. Google's Privacy Policy is available at:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. Children's Privacy

Our games, applications, and websites are intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age required by applicable law.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

8. Contact

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you may contact us at doneeercorporation@gmail.com.
