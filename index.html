Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 19, 2026

DoneeerCorporation ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and disclose information when you use our mobile applications and games.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect or store any personal information (such as your name, address, or email) directly.

Game Progress & Local Data: We save your game progress locally on your device using Unity’s PlayerPrefs feature (or local storage). This data stays on your device and is not shared with us or any third parties.

Advertising Data: We use Google AdMob as our advertising partner across our apps. AdMob may collect certain information, such as your device's advertising ID (AAID), IP address, and other technical identifiers, to provide personalized ads and measure ad performance. You can manage your ad preferences or opt-out of personalized advertising through your device's settings.

2. How We Use Information

The information collected by our advertising partner (Google AdMob) is used to:

  • Display advertisements within the apps and games.
  • Measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns.
  • Provide a better gaming and user experience.

3. Data Retention

We do not store any personal data on our servers. Game progress and app data are stored locally on your device and can be deleted by you at any time by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling the game/application.

4. Third-Party Services

Our applications integrate the Google AdMob SDK. You can review Google’s privacy policy here: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Children's Privacy

Our applications are intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within this document.
